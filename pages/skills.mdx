# Skills

> 1 Primary Skill, 3 Secondary Skills

## Primary Skills

#### Mage

**Starting HP :** 1d8 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d8\
**Weapons & Armor :** All one-handed swords, daggers, and staves\
Can use **_Arcane Magic_** with the **_Spell Attribute_** of **_INT_**

#### Oathbound

**Starting HP :** 1d10 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d10\
**Weapons & Armor :** All blunt weapons, Light, Medium, Heavy Armor, all helmets, all shields\
Can use **_Divine Magic_** with the **_Spell Attribute_** of **_WIS_** having made an Oath to a **_Higher Power_**. Start with a **_Prayer Book_** containing 3 **_Miracles_** appropriate to your **_Higher Power_**.

#### Action Surge

**Starting HP :** 1d12 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting (HD):** 1d12 with **_Advantage_**\
**Weapons & Armor :** Any and All\
You can make 1 Attack / Level as an Action.

#### **Rage**

**Starting HP :** 1d12 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting (HD):** 1d12 with **_Advantage_**\

Enter a **_Rage_** without using an action. While in a **_Rage_** your Attacks and Damage gain **_Advantage_**. Damage dealt to you is also made with **_Advantage_**.

While in a **_Rage_**, you cannot be charmed and enemies have disadvantage on tests of Morale.

After a **_Moment_** where you do not attack/are not attacked, the **_Rage_** ends and the **_Usage Die_** is rolled.

| Level | Rage Usage Die |
| :---- | :------------- |
| 1     | Ud4            |
| 3     | Ud6            |
| 6     | Ud8            |
| 9     | Ud10           |

#### Sneak Attack

**Starting HP :** 1d10 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d10\
**Weapons & Armor :** All one-handed swords, bows, daggers, Light Armor, Medium Armor, small shields\
Roll with **_Advantage_** when attacking from behind and deals an rolls an additional weapon damage die + Level damage.

#### Pactbound

Can use **_Divine Magic_** with the **_Spell Attribute_** of **_CHA_**, having made a Pact with a **_Higher Power_**. Start with a **_Prayer Book_** containing 3 **_Invocations (Miracles)_** appropriate to your **_Higher Power_**.
