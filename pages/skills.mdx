# Skills
> 1 Primary Skill, 3 Secondary Skills

## Primary Skills

**Mage**\
Can use **_Arcane Magic_** with the **_Spell Attribute_** of **_INT_**

**Oathbound**\
Can use **_Divine Magic_** with the **_Spell Attribute_** of **_INT_** having made an Oath to a Higher Power.

**Action Surge**\
You can make 1 Attack / Level as an Action. not hit the same target twice in a row.

**Rage**\
Enter a **_Rage_** without using an action. While in a **_Rage_**, your Attack Damage die upgrades by one level (d10/d8) and you gain advantage on attack and damage rolls.

While in a **_Rage_**, you cannot be charmed and enemies have disadvantage on tests of Morale.

After a **_Moment_** where the Berserker does not attack/is not attacked, the **_Rage_** ends and the **_Usage Die_** is rolled.

| Level | Rage Usage Die |
| :---- | :------------- |
| 1     | Ud4            |
| 3     | Ud6            |
| 6     | Ud8            |
| 9     | Ud10           |

**Sneak Attack**\
Rolls with ***Advantage*** when attacking from behind and deals an rolls an additional weapon damage die + Level damage.
