# Skills

> 1 Primary Skill, 3 Secondary Skills

## Primary Skills

#### Mage

Start with 2 MD.

#### Cleric

Start with 2 FD and a **_Prayer Book_** containing 3 **_Miracles_** appropriate to your **_Higher Power_**.

#### Martialist

Start with 1 **_Grit_** and knowledge of 3 **_Techniques_**

#### Rogue

Start with 1 **_Grit_**, knowledge of 2 **_Techniques_**, and the **Sneak Attack** skill.

**Sneak Attack :** Roll with **_Advantage_** when attacking from behind, double the rolled damage die.

#### Spellblade

Start with 1 MD and knowledge of 2 **_Techniques_**. These **_Techniques_** can be powered by MD instead of **_Grit_**.

#### Oathbound

Start with 1 FD and knowledge of 1 **_Technique_** and 2 **_Miracles_**. These **_Techniques_** can be powered by FD instead of **_Grit_**.

#### Sorcerer

Start with 2MD and the **_Spell Attribute_** of **_CON_**

#### Pactbound

Start with 2 FD and the **_Spell Attribute_** of **_CHA_**, having made a Pact with a **_Eldritch Power_**. Start with a **_Book_** containing 3 **_Invocations (Miracles)_** appropriate to your **_Higher Power_**.
