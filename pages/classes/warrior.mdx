# Warrior

> You are a master of the arts of combat, you know how to survive in a fight.

**Starting HP :** 1d12 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting (HD):** 1d12

| d6  | What do bring on your travels?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| :-- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | A **drakeskin heraldic cape**, bearing your crest. Its unique lining can half the damage of frost or fire.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 2   | A **letter of marque**, establishing your noble rank. Can be used to gain advantage on certain **CHA** checks.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 3   | Your trusted **riding steed** - 1HD, Kick 1d6, Saddlebags (+4 inventory slots). When riding your horse, you can move to **Far Away** during your **Turn**.                                                                                                                               |
| 4   | Your **heavy crossbow and quiver of bolts** (Ammo Ud8). It has served you well on the road. You are so familiar with its workings that you can choose to hit automatically once per day.                                                                                                 |
| 5   | An enchanted **ancestral blade** (longsword or greatsword) that warriors in your family have long carried into battle. When using it, you can reroll one missed attack per combat.                                                                                                       |
| 6   | The **bronze amulet** passed down through your family. An ancient version of your family crest is carved into the disc of the amulet. If you were to fall OofA and die, the amulet revives you to half of your max HP instead. After use in this way, the amulet vanishes into thin air. |

## Equipment

Every Warrior starts with 3d6 coins and one of the following sets of equipment:\
A. Military Gambeson (1 AP), a long sword, large kite shield (2 AP), unopened orders, wine skin (Ud6)\
B. Well-worn Studded Leather Armor (2 AP), iron helm with visor (1 AP), Glaive (two-handed, _Bulky_), tobacco pouch (Ud6) & pipe, dice set.

## Martialist

The Warrior spirit compels you to excellence in a fight. Start with 1 **_Grit_** (Ud6) and knowledge of the following **_Techniques_**:

Roll **_Grit_** to use these techniques with [Dice] being the amount of Dice rolled and [Sum] equaling the total number rolled.

| Technique         | Description                                                           |
| :---------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Second Wind :** | Regain [Sum] HP                                                       |
| **Parry :**       | As a reaction against a Melee Attack, the attacker takes [Sum] Damage |
| **Surge :**       | As part of your action, you may make [Dice] additional Attacks        |
