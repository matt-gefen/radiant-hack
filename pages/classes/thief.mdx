# Thief
> You are a master of stealth, excelling at acrobatics and deception.

**Starting HP :** 1d10 + ***CON***\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d10\
**Weapons & Armor :** All one-handed swords, bows, daggers, gambeson, leather, small shields\

| d6   | What have you stolen?                                                                                                                                                             |
| :--- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | A ceramic bottle of your old partner's homebrewed **knockout gas**. Throw bottle to release gas. All ***Nearby*** make a ***CON*** save or fall unconscious.                   |
| 2    | A **rune stone**, taken from a witch of the wood. Ud10. Harness it's power to cast either the **[Charm](/magic/spells/arcane_spells#charm)** or the **[Rusting Grasp](/magic/spells/arcane_spells#rusting_grasp)** spells. |
| 3    | A pouch of **"Catnip"**. Ud8. Take to gain advantage on DEX Tests for a 1d4 turns.                                                                                             |
| 4    | Your lucky **dagger of returning**. A throwing dagger that returns to your hand if it misses its target.                                                                           |
| 5    | The **porcelain mask** taken from a masquerade that allows one to shroud themselves in a **[Glamour](/magic/spells/arcane_spells#glamour)**. Ud8.                                                               |
| 6    | A crystal **skeleton key** taken from the Lord of Thieves that can be used to open any lock. It crumbles into dust after use.                                                       |

## Equipment
Every Thief starts with a **Disguise** of their choosing and one of the following sets of equipment:\
A - Black leather hood & vest (2 AP), 2 short swords, 2d8 counterfeit coins, lockpicks.\
B - Cloth gambeson (1 AP), bow & arrows (Ud8), 4d6 coins, a hooded lantern & a flask of oil.

## Skills

**Dungeoneering :** Roll with ***Advantage*** when testing ***DEX*** to avoid damage or effects from traps and magical devices.

**Sneak Attack :** Rolls with ***Advantage*** when attacking from behind and deals 2d6 / 2d4 + the Thief's level damage.

**Thief Training :** Rolls with ***Advantage*** when performing delicate tasks, climbing, hearing sounds, moving silently, understanding written languages and opening locks.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if attributes increase, roll twice for ***DEX*** *or* ***WIS***.
