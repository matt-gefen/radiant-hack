# Mage

> Through study, bloodline, or simply enough exposure, you are able to tap into the nearly limitless well of potential that is **_Arcane Magic_**.

**Starting HP :** 1d8 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d8

## Equipment

Every Mage starts with 3d6 coins, torches, rations, a toolkit of their choice, and one of the following sets of equipment:

A. Heavy cloth robes (1 AP) and wide brimmed hat, bent oak staff, iron dagger, book detailing magical phenomena\
B. Embroidered travel tunic and warded wool cloak (1 AP), wyrmwood wand, shortsword, leatherbound journals.

## Class Features

### Arcane Magic

> Level 1 Feature

You start with 2 **_Mana_** and use **_INT_** as your **_Spellcasting Attribute_**. See rules for **_[Spellcasting](/magic/spellcasting)_**.

### Familiar

> Level 2 Feature

At Level 2, you can bind a spirit to serve as their **_Familiar_**. **_Familiars_** are intelligent and can understand most commands. This **_Familiar_** can take the shape of a small mundane animal and can change shape as an **_Action_**.

You can share the senses of their **_Familiar_** at will as well as communicate with them telepathically. A **_Familiar_** cannot Attack but can assist with Stunts in combat. If a Familiar is taken **_OofA_**, they will reform themselves over the next **_Respite_**.

At Level 5, you can cast spells originating from your **_Familiar_** so long as they are **_Nearby_**.

### Arcane Soul

> Level 3 Feature

Your experience allows you to use your own life force as fuel for your spells. Convert 2d6 HP into a single **_Mana_** to be used to power your magic. If you do not have the rolled HP, the spell fails. You can use this ability multiple times on a single spell, until rolling a cost that cannot be paid or hitting the max **_Mana_** that can be used (4).

At Level 5, this cost is reduced to 1d6 HP per **_Mana_**.

### Attunement

> Level 5 Feature

Attuning to **_Mana_** has given you a sixth sense which allows you to detect its presence. Once a day, you can do one of the following:

- Concentrate to sense any sources of magic **_Nearby_**. This includes inside objects and through walls.
- Immediately understand the workings of a spell or magical ability.

You gain an additional daily use of this power each Level after acquiring it.

### Arcane Link

> Level 7 Feature

Connection with your well of magical power allows you to bind your **_Mana_** onto others.

By touching a **_Creature_** and expending **_Mana_**, you can share their senses as you would your **_Familiar_**. They are not aware of your presence.

The link is canceled when you use this ability again, and while you’re concentrating on it, you cannot do anything yourself except sit in quiet focus, otherwise the effect breaks.

1 Mana: The link lasts [Sum] Hours.\
2 Mana: The link lasts [Sum] Days.\
3 Mana: The link lasts indefinitely.\
4 Mana: The link lasts indefinitely and requires no concentration.

To establish a link with another **_Character_**, they must be willing, and you may need to Test to be able to touch a hostile, unaware, or unwilling character.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if **_Attributes_** increase, roll twice for **_INT_** _or_ **_WIS_**.

Gain 1 **_Mana_**
