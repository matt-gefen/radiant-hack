# Mage

> Through study, bloodline, or simply enough exposure, you are able to tap into the nearly limitless well of potential that is **_Arcane Magic_**.

**Starting HP :** 1d8 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d8

## Equipment

Every Mage starts with 3d6 coins, torches, rations, a toolkit of their choice, and one of the following sets of equipment:

A. Heavy cloth robes (1 AP) and wide brimmed hat, bent oak staff, iron dagger, book detailing magical phenomena\
B. Embroidered travel tunic and warded wool cloak (1 AP), wyrmwood wand, shortsword, leatherbound journals.

## Class Features

### Arcane Magic

> Level 1 Feature

You start with 2 **_Mana_** and use **_INT_** as your **_Spellcasting Attribute_**. See rules for **_[Spellcasting](/magic/spellcasting)_**.

### Familiar

> Level 2 Feature

At Level 2, you can bind a spirit to serve as their **_Familiar_**. **_Familiars_** are intelligent and can understand most commands. This **_Familiar_** can take the shape of a small mundane animal and can change shape as an **_Action_**.

You can share the senses of their **_Familiar_** at will as well as communicate with them telepathically. A **_Familiar_** cannot Attack but can assist with Stunts in combat. If a Familiar is taken **_OofA_**, they will reform themselves over the next **_Respite_**.

At Level 5, you can cast spells originating from your **_Familiar_** so long as they are **_Nearby_**.

### Attunement

> Level 3 Feature

Attuning to **_Mana_** has given you a sixth sense which allows you to detect its presence. Once a day, you can concentrate to sense any sources of magic **_Nearby_**. This includes inside objects and through walls.

You gain an additional daily use of this power at 4th and 6th Level.

### Arcane Soul

> Level 5 Feature

Your experience allows you to use your own life force as fuel for your spells. Convert 2d6 HP into a single **_Mana_** to be used to fuel your magic. You can use this ability multiple times on a single spell.

At Level 7, this cost is reduced to 1d6 HP per **_Mana_**.

### Focused Mind

> Level 7 Feature

You no longer need a Focus to effectively manage your **_Mana_**. **_Mana_** is now only consumed on a 1 and 2.

At Level 9, **_Mana_** is only consumed on a 1.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if **_Attributes_** increase, roll twice for **_INT_** _or_ **_WIS_**.

Gain 1 **_Mana_**
