# Mage

> You are the scholar of the arcane, handling spells and magic like no one else.

**Starting HP :** 1d8 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d8

## Equipment

Every Mage starts with 3d6 coins and one of the following sets of equipment:\
A. Heavy cloth robes (1 AP) and wide brimmed hat, bent oak staff, iron dagger, book detailing magical phenomena.\
B. Embroidered travel tunic, Enchanted wool cloak (1 AP), wyrmwood wand, shortsword, leatherbound journals with quill & ink.

| d6  | What do you specialize in?                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| :-- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Abjuration**: You wear a **ring of spell protection** that marks your research. The wearer can choose to negate the effects of a spell that targets them. Ud4.                                                                                    |
| 2   | **Divination**: You carry a small **silver scrying mirror** spelled to allow the mage to fleetingly view any place or object they've seen before. Ud10.                                                                                             |
| 3   | **Evocation**: You have earned an **inscribed emerald circlet** granting +1 MD.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 4   | **Illusion**: You have a **crystal prism** that refracts light. Activate the prism to inflict blindness on up to d4 targets for d6 rounds. Ud8.                                                                                                     |
| 5   | **Transmutation**: You wear enchanted **half-moon spectacles**. Wearing these glasses allows the wearer to enhance their vision to either _Detect Magic_ or _Detect Invisibility_ once per day.                                                     |
| 6   | **Conjuration**: A deal with an outer being has left you with a **binding rune** tattooed on your skin. Use the binding rune to summon a lesser demon or minor fey to do a single task within their power. The tattoo becomes inert after this use. |

## Mage

Mages through study and understanding are able to tap into **_Arcane Magic_**. 

### Level 1

Mages start with 2 **_Magic Die_**. See rules for **_[Magic](/magic)_** and **_[Arcane Magic](/magic/arcane)_**.

To cast **_Arcane Magic_**, a Mage needs to be holding a **_Focus_** like a wand or staff. A **_Focus_** always takes at least one hand slot to use.

## Additional Skills

**Arcane Reflexes:** Roll with **_Advantage_** when testing **_INT_** to avoid damage or effects from spells or magical devices.
**Arcane Aptitude :** When rolling to see if a **_Magic Die_** has been expended, Mages may add 1d4 to the roll.
**Learned :** You’ve enjoyed a broad general education in a topic of choice, such as history, politics, biology, or philosophy.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if **_Attributes_** increase, roll twice for **_INT_** _or_ **_WIS_**.
