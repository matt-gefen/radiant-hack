# Mage

> You are the scholar of the arcane, handling spells and magic like no one else.

**Starting HP :** 1d8 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d8

## Equipment

Every Mage starts with 3d6 coins, torches, rations, a toolkit of their choice, and one of the following sets of equipment:\
A. Heavy cloth robes (1 AP) and wide brimmed hat, bent oak staff (Arcane Focus), iron dagger, book detailing magical phenomena, and a .\
B. Embroidered travel tunic and warded wool cloak (1 AP), wyrmwood wand (Arcane Focus), shortsword, leatherbound journals, and a .

## Features

### Arcane Magic

> Level 1 Feature

Mages through study and understanding are able to tap into **_Arcane Magic_**.

Mages start with 2 **_Magic Die_**. See rules for **_[Magic](/magic)_** and **_[Arcane Magic](/magic/arcane)_**.

## Additional Skills

**Arcane Reflexes:** Roll with **_Advantage_** when testing **_INT_** to avoid damage or effects from spells or magical devices.
**Arcane Aptitude :** When rolling to see if a **_Magic Die_** has been expended, Mages may add 1d4 to the roll.
**Learned :** You’ve enjoyed a broad general education in a topic of choice, such as history, politics, biology, or philosophy.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if **_Attributes_** increase, roll twice for **_INT_** _or_ **_WIS_**.

Gain 1 **_Magic Dice_**
