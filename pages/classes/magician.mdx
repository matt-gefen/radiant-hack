# Magician 

> You are the scholar of the arcane, handling spells and magic like no one else.

**Starting HP :** d4 + 4\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d4\
**Weapons & Armor :** 1-handed Sword and Staff\
**Attack Damage :** 1d4 / 1 Unarmed or Improvising

| d6   | What do you specialize in?                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| :--- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | **Abjuration**: You wear a **ring of spell protection** that marks your research. The wearer can choose to negate the effects of a spell that targets them. Ud4.                                                                                    |
| 2    | **Divination**: You carry a small **silver scrying mirror** spelled to allow the magician to fleetingly view any place or object they've seen before. Ud4.                                                                                                    |
| 3    | **Evocation**: You wield a well-used **Duelist's wand**. Use to roll spell damage with advantage. Ud6. |
| 4    | **Illusion**: You have a **crystal prism** that refracts light. Activate the prism to inflict blindness on up to d4 targets for d6 rounds. Ud6.                                                                                                    |
| 5    | **Transmutation**: You wear an enchanted black **star silk cloak**. Wearing this cloak allows the wearer to enhance their vision to either *Detect Magic* or *Detect Invisibility* once per day.                                                    |
| 6    | **Conjuration**: A deal with an outer being has left you with a **binding rune** tattooed on your skin. Use the binding rune to summon a lesser demon or minor fey to do a single task within their power. The tattoo becomes inert after this use. |

## Equipment
Every Magician starts with one of the following sets of equipment:\
A. Cloth robes (1 Armour) and wide brimmed hat, bent oak staff, short sword, book detailing magical phenomena, 2d8 coins & purse.\
B. Embroidered travel tunic, Enchanted wool cloak (1 Armour), wyrmwood wand, iron dagger, leatherbound journals with quill & ink, 4d6 coins & purse.

## Spellbook

Magicians start with a large spellbook containing a total of 1d4+2 Arcane spells from the Level 1 and 2 starting spell lists below.

Magicians have knowledge of the following minor spells at will:
* **Prestidigitation :** Create a harmless magical effect.
* **Mage Hand :** Lift/move something 5 lbs or less within a Nearby range. Once the hand moves out of range, it vanishes and the object falls.

## Special features

Roll with Advantage when testing INT to avoid damage or effects from spells or magical devices.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if attributes increase, roll twice for **INT** *or* **WIS**.

Magicians can cast a number of [Arcane Spells](/magic/arcane_spells) per day. The Magician's **Spell Attribute** is **INT**. See the rules for [Spellcasting](/rules#spellcasting)

### Magician starting spells

**Level 1**

* **Burning Hands :** Deal 1d4 fire damage/caster level to 1d4 Creatures in Close range.
* **Charm :** A Nearby target obeys a simple command and will perform a simple Action.
* **Detect Magic :** Everything Nearby that is magic glows - Ud6 Minutes.
* **Identify :** Study an object for 1 minute to learn its properties and how to use it. Also reveals any magic or spells affecting it.
* **Light :** Create dim light from a Nearby spot or object - Ud8 Minutes.
* **Magic Missile :** A Far-Away or Distant target takes 1d6 damage/caster level.
* **Ray of Frost :** A Close or Nearby target 1d4 frost damage/caster level. This target's is **stuck** for a Moment.
* **Shield :** Gives the caster Arcane Mail (+2 Armour) - Ud6 Moments.
* **Sleep :** Puts 4d6 HD 'worth' of beings to sleep - Ud6 Minutes.

**Level 2**

* **Acid Arrow :** 1 Nearby or Far Away target takes 1d6/level damage now and take **Ongoing Damage**.
* **Darkness :** Pure darkness covers a Nearby area and blocks all types of vision - lasts Ud6 Minutes.
* **Detect Magic :** Everything Nearby that is magic glows - Ud6 minutes.
* **Detect Invisibility :** Invisible creatures or items Nearby are revealed to the caster.
* **Invisibility :** A nearby creature is made invisible until it attacks or dispelled.
* **Knock/Lock :** A Nearby door or lock is either opened or locked.
* **Levitate :** The caster floats up to 6 feet from the ground - Ud12 Minutes.
* **Locate Object :** Name an object and know its direction if within 1000 ft. 
* **Mirror Image :** Create 1d4+1 illusory duplicates around the caster. While duplicates exist, the caster has advantage on tests to avoid damage. Each successful hit destroys one image. 
* **Ray of Enfeedblement :** A Close or Nearby target has disadvantage on **STR** tests and deals half damage with weapons. Ud8.
* **Shocking Grasp :** Touch deals 1d6/level damage to a single target. Metal-armored targets can't avoid the damage.
* **Web :**  Traps a Nearby area, stopping movement - lasts Ud6 Minutes.
