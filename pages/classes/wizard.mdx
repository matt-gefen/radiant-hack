# Wizard 

> You are the scholar of the arcane, handling spells and magic like no one else.

**Starting HP :** 1d8 + ***CON***\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d8\
**Weapons & Armor :** All one-handed swords, daggers, and staves\
**Spell Attribute :** ***INT***

| d6   | What do you specialize in?                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| :--- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | **Abjuration**: You wear a **ring of spell protection** that marks your research. The wearer can choose to negate the effects of a spell that targets them. Ud4.                                                                                    |
| 2    | **Divination**: You carry a small **silver scrying mirror** spelled to allow the wizard to fleetingly view any place or object they've seen before. Ud10.                                                                                           |
| 3    | **Evocation**: You have earned an **inscribed emerald ring** granting +1 SD.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 4    | **Illusion**: You have a **crystal prism** that refracts light. Activate the prism to inflict blindness on up to d4 targets for d6 rounds. Ud8.                                                                                                     |
| 5    | **Transmutation**: You wear enchanted **half-moon spectacles**. Wearing these glasses allows the wearer to enhance their vision to either *Detect Magic* or *Detect Invisibility* once per day.                                                    |
| 6    | **Conjuration**: A deal with an outer being has left you with a **binding rune** tattooed on your skin. Use the binding rune to summon a lesser demon or minor fey to do a single task within their power. The tattoo becomes inert after this use. |

## Equipment
Every Wizard starts with one of the following sets of equipment:\
A. Cloth robes (1 AP) and wide brimmed hat, bent oak staff, short sword, book detailing magical phenomena, 2d8 coins & purse.\
B. Embroidered travel tunic, Enchanted wool cloak (1 AP), wyrmwood wand, iron dagger, leatherbound journals with quill & ink, 4d6 coins & purse.

## Arcane Magic

Wizards through study and understanding are able to tap into Arcane Magic. Wizards have ***Spell Die*** equal to their ***INT***. See rules for ***[Magic](/magic)*** and ***[Arcane Magic](/magic/arcane)***.

## Special features

Roll with Advantage when testing ***INT*** to avoid damage or effects from spells or magical devices.

When rolling to see if a ***Spell Die*** has been expended, Wizards may add 1d4 to the roll.

Wizards can cast **Magecraft** at will to create a harmless magical effect or Lift/move something 5 lbs or less within a Nearby range. Once the object is moved out of range, it falls.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if attributes increase, roll twice for ***INT*** *or* ***WIS***.
