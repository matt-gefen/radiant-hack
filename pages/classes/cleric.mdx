# Cleric

> You are a servant of a diety, by carrying out their will you can channel their power.

**Starting HP :** 1d10 + ***CON***\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d10\
**Weapons & Armor :** All blunt weapons, cloth, leather, mail, all helmets, all shields\
**Spell Attribute :** ***WIS***

## Equipment
Every Cleric starts with one of the following sets of equipment:\
A - Studded hide breastplate (2 AP), flail, a small shield (1 AP), 2d8 coins and purse, 6 Torches (Ud6 per torch), flint & steel.\
B - Thick cloth vestments (1 AP), two handed hammer, 1d10 coins, a small glass bottle of holy water.

## Prayer book

Clerics start with a ***Prayer Book*** containing a total of 1d4 divine prayers from the Level 1 and 2 Cleric starting prayers lists.

## Special features

Roll with Advantage when testing ***CON*** to avoid damage from poison or being paralyzed.

### Banishing undead

Clerics can attempt to banish all ***Nearby*** undead as an action. They must successfully test their ***WIS*** for each group of creatures they are attempting to banish, adding the creature's ***HD*** to the roll. A GM will determine which creatures are in any particular group.

Undead monsters that are **Banished** by Clerics must spend all their movement (and convert actions to movement) to move away from the Cleric for 2d4 ***Moments*** after being **Banished**.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if attributes increase, roll twice for ***STR*** or ***WIS***.


## Divine Magic

Clerics can cast a number of [Miracles](/magic/divine_prayers) per day. The Cleric's **Spell Attribute** is **WIS**. See the rules for [Spellcasting](/rules#spellcasting).
