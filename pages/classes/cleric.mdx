# Cleric

> You are a servant of a diety, by carrying out their will you can channel their power.

**Starting HP :** 1d10 + ***CON***\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d10\
**Weapons & Armor :** All blunt weapons, cloth, leather, mail, all helmets, all shields\
**Spell Attribute :** ***WIS***

## Equipment
Every Cleric starts with a ***Focus*** (*petty*) and one of the following sets of equipment:\
A - Studded hide breastplate (2 AP), flail, a small shield (1 AP), 2d8 coins and purse, 6 Torches (Ud6 per torch), flint & steel.\
B - Thick cloth vestments (1 AP), two handed hammer, 1d10 coins, a small glass bottle of holy water.

## Skills

***Sturdy :*** Roll with ***Advantage*** when testing ***CON*** to avoid damage from poison or being ***Paralyzed***.

***Mediator :*** +1 on reaction rolls for those you share a language with.

***Divine Rebuke :*** Channel the **Rebuke** Miracle with +1 SD.  The ***Character*** can do this ***WIS*** times each day.

## Divine Magic

Clerics through their devotion to a ***Higher Power*** are granted the ability to channel ***Divine Magic*** and call upon ***Miracles***. Clerics have ***Spell Die*** equal to their ***WIS*** (their ***Spellcasting Attribute***). See rules for ***[Magic](/magic)*** and ***[Divine Magic](/magic/divine)***.

### Prayer book

Clerics start with a ***Prayer Book*** containing the following ***Miracles***:

| Miracle | Description | 
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rebuke** | The caster targets [Sum] ***Enemies of Faith***. For each, the caster must make a DR12 - [Dice] WIS Test. On a success, they will flee in fear.|
| **Bless** | The caster declares the allies they want to bless and rolls SD. The total value of [Sum] becomes a Pool of points that those blessed can tap into at will when making Tests. The Pool lasts until they next ***Rest***. The moment an ally needs to roll a Test, they can use up a number of points from the Pool to temporarily raise a single Attribute. The Pool must be tapped into before rolling the Test. |
| **Replenish** | The caster targets [Dice] allies, who regain [Sum] HP each, not exceeding their maximum HP. This means that a roll of 2 SD, with results of 4 and 6, allows 2 allies to regain 10 HP each. |

## Leveling up

Roll to see if attributes increase, roll twice for ***STR*** or ***WIS***.
