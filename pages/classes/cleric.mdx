# Cleric

> You are a servant of a **_Higher Power_**, by carrying out their will you are able channel their **_Powers_**.

**Starting HP :** 1d10 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d10

## Class Features

### Powers

> Level 1 Feature

Clerics start with 1 **_Mana_**, a **_Focus_**, and use **_WIS_** as their **_Spellcasting Attribute_**. See rules for **_[Magic](/magic)_**.

Clerics are not subject to risk of **_Arcane Feedback_** when using their Mana for **_Powers_**. Additionally, their **_Focus_** does not take up an inventory slot and can be a ring, a necklace, a bracelet with prayer beads, sacred tattoos, or another type of trinket. Many gods are petty and require a devotee to carry their **_Focus_** out in the open, identifiable by all. Hiding one's allegiance is frowned upon by **_Higher Powers_**.

Clerics start with the following **_Powers_**. **_Powers_** may require investment of **_Mana_** to use or be passive abilities granted to the Cleric.

| Powers        | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| :------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Rebuke**    | Target [Sum] **_Enemies of Faith_**. Each target must make a DR12 + [Mana] WIS Test. On a success, they will flee in fear.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Replenish** | Target [Mana] allies, who regain [Sum] HP each, not exceeding their maximum HP. Example: Investing 2 Mana, with results of 4 and 6, allows 2 allies to regain 10 HP each.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Bless**     | Target [Mana] allies they want to bless. The total value of [Sum] becomes a Pool of points that those blessed can tap into at will when making Tests. The Pool lasts until they next **_Rest_**. The moment an ally needs to roll a Test, they can use up a number of points from the Pool to temporarily raise a single **_Attribute_**. The Pool must be tapped into before rolling the Test. |

### Powers

Each Level, you learn one additional **_Power_** of your choice.

| Powers       | Description                                                                                            |
| :----------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mediator** | +[Mana] on a reaction roll.                                                                            |
| **Sturdy**   | Roll with **_Advantage_** when testing **_CON_** to avoid damage from poison or being **_Paralyzed_**. |

## Leveling up

Roll to see if **_Attributes_** increase, roll twice for **_STR_** or **_WIS_**.

Gain 1 **_Mana_**
