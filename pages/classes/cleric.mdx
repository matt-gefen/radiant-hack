# Cleric

> You are a servant of a diety, by carrying out their will you can channel their power.

**Starting HP :** 1d10 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d10

## Equipment

Every Cleric starts with 3d6 coins, torches, rations, a toolkit of their choice, and one of the following sets of equipment:\
A - Studded hide armor and brass breastplate (1 AP), flail, a small shield (+1 AP)
B - Thick cloth vestments (1 AP), a war hammer

| d6  | What diety do you serve?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| :-- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Auoua, Keeper of Mysteries and Lady of the Moon**: Take a **silver crescent pendant**. Wielder can spend a charge to cast **Invisibility** on a target of their choice.                                                                                                              |
| 2   | **Njǫrd, Lord of Wind and Sea**: You have a **whale bone scrimshaw** depicting part of an ancient saga. Recount the tale aloud to summon a lesser elemental of water, air, ice, or lightning under your control. The scrimshaw melts as if made of ice once used in this way.          |
| 3   | **Calico, The Lucky**: Those who follow Calico find a **lucky copper coin**. Flip the coin and roll with **_Advantage_** once per day. The coin has a 1/6 chance of vanishing from your pocket.                                                                                        |
| 4   | **Gaia, the Earth Titan**: Followers wear a blessed **stone ring**. Use a charge to transmute your skin to stone (+3 AP) for 1d6 Minutes.                                                                                                                                              |
| 5   | **Shepherd of the Dead**: Guides of this faith wear an **amulet** shaped like a shepherd's crook. The wielder may ask a question of the dead whenever they chose. After doing so, the wielder takes **_Disadvantage_** on the next test they make. The dead may not answer truthfully. |
| 6   | **Sulis, Goddess of the Sun**: Carry with you a **small sundial** bearing the face of Sulis. Can be used once a day to shed light that behaves like a torch.                                                                                                                           |

## Features

### Divine Magic

> Level 1 Feature

Clerics through their devotion to a **_Higher Power_** are granted the ability to channel **_Divine Magic_** and call upon **_Miracles_**.\
Clerics start with 1 **_Faith Dice_**, a **_Focus_**, and use **_WIS_** as their **_Spellcasting Attribute_**.

See rules for **_[Magic](/magic)_** and **_[Divine Magic](/magic/divine)_**.

Clerics start with knowledge of the following **_Miracles_**:

| Miracle       | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| :------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Rebuke**    | The caster targets [Sum] **_Enemies of Faith_**. For each, the caster must make a DR12 - [Dice] WIS Test. On a success, they will flee in fear.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Bless**     | The caster declares the allies they want to bless and rolls MD. The total value of [Sum] becomes a Pool of points that those blessed can tap into at will when making Tests. The Pool lasts until they next **_Rest_**. The moment an ally needs to roll a Test, they can use up a number of points from the Pool to temporarily raise a single Attribute. The Pool must be tapped into before rolling the Test. |
| **Replenish** | The caster targets [Dice] allies, who regain [Sum] HP each, not exceeding their maximum HP. This means that a roll of 2 MD, with results of 4 and 6, allows 2 allies to regain 10 HP each.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

## Additional Skills

**Sturdy :** Roll with **_Advantage_** when testing **_CON_** to avoid damage from poison or being **_Paralyzed_**.
**Mediator :** +1 on reaction rolls for those you share a language with.
**Divine Rebuke :** Channel the **Rebuke** Miracle with +1 MD. You can do this **_WIS_** times each day.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if **_Attributes_** increase, roll twice for **_STR_** or **_WIS_**.

Gain 1 **_Faith Dice_**
