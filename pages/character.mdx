# Creating your character
### Character concept
The first step to creating a memorable ***Character*** is coming up with an engaging ‘concept’. In your mind, think of one sentence that summarises who the ***Character*** is, and what might make them interesting to play.

Start with a simple sentence that combines:
- What they do (their [***Class***](#choose-a-class) or profession)
- A defining trait
- A simple goal or something that drives them to adventure

**Examples:**
- An obsessive wizard seeking after ancient lost magic
- A smooth talking trickster on the run from a noble they conned
- A wandering mercenary searching for their missing friend
- A gambler in a debt to the wrong people, taking dangerous jobs for pay
- A priest who wants to serve the common folk rather than the temple hierarchy

***Characters*** are Human by default but can be other [***Species***](/species) of humanoid. Non-humanoids cannot be played in this ruleset.

### Attributes
Every ***Character*** has 6 ***Attributes*** that define them. They define everything about the ***Character*** - how strong, dexterous, resilient, intelligent, wise, and charismatic they are. 
- Take 3d6 (3 six sided dice) and roll them, adding them together. Do this for each of the ***Attributes*** below in order. Using the table below, find the Attribute Score associated with the total. Write the results in pencil, next to the ***Attributes*** on a Character sheet.
-  If you roll and get a score of +3, don’t roll dice for the next ***Attribute*** - instead it will be -1. Roll again as per normal after this.
   
| Attribute          |                                            |
| :----------------- | :----------------------------------------- |
| Strength (STR)     | Physical power and **Melee Attacks**       |
| Dexterity (DEX)    | Swiftness, agility, and **Ranged Attacks** |
| Constitution (CON) | Resilience and physical well being         |
| Intelligence (INT) | Knowledge, judgement, and **Arcane Magic** |
| Wisdom (WIS)       | Cunning, perception, and **Divine Magic**  |
| Charisma (CHA)     | Influence and the power of personality     |

| Total Roll | Attribute Score |
| :--------- | :-------------: |
| 1-4        |       -3        |
| 5-6        |       -2        |
| 7-8        |       -1        |
| 9-12       |        0        |
| 13-14      |       +1        |
| 15-16      |       +2        |
| 17-20      |       +3        |

You may swap 2 ***Attributes*** around if that would better fit your ***Character*** idea. Your ***Class*** choice will also add additional modifiers to your ***Attributes***.

## Choose a class

Your ***Class*** represents your ***Character***'s profession and calling - their primary role in the ***Party***. 

***Classes*** define what your character excels at and offers different ways to solve problems and face danger, whether through steel, spells, stealth, or sacred power.

**A class will determine**
- A ***Character's*** specialized skills
- How much damage a starting ***Character*** can take (***Hit Points***)
- What armor and weapons they can use without additional training
- What they gain as they advance in Levels
- Starting equipment and coins

| Core Classes                | Description                                                          | Complexity (1-5) |
