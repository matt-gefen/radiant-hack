# Creating your character

## Character concept

The first step to creating a memorable **_Character_** is coming up with an engaging ‘concept’. In your mind, think of one sentence that summarises who the **_Character_** is, and what might make them interesting to play.

Start with a simple sentence that combines:

- What they do (their [**_Class_**](#choose-a-class) or profession)
- A defining trait
- A simple goal or something that drives them to adventure

**Examples:**

- An obsessive wizard seeking after ancient lost magic
- A smooth talking trickster on the run from a noble they conned
- A wandering mercenary searching for their missing friend
- A gambler in a debt to the wrong people, taking dangerous jobs for pay
- A priest who wants to serve the common folk rather than the temple hierarchy

**_Characters_** are Human by default but can be other [**_Species_**](/species) of humanoid.

## Attributes

Every **_Character_** has 6 **_Attributes_** that define them. They define everything about the **_Character's_** abilities, strengths, and weaknesses.

### Rolling attributes

- Take 3d6 (3 six sided dice) and roll them, adding them together. Do this for each of the **_Attributes_** below in order. Using the table below, find the Attribute Score associated with the total. Write the results in pencil, next to the **_Attributes_** on a Character sheet.
- If you roll and get a score of +3, don’t roll dice for the next **_Attribute_** - instead it will be -1. Roll again as per normal after this.

| Attribute          | Description                                |
| :----------------- | :----------------------------------------- |
| Strength (STR)     | Physical power and **Melee Attacks**       |
| Dexterity (DEX)    | Swiftness, agility, and **Ranged Attacks** |
| Constitution (CON) | Resilience and physical well being         |
| Intelligence (INT) | Knowledge, judgement, and **Arcane Magic** |
| Wisdom (WIS)       | Cunning, perception, and **Divine Magic**  |
| Charisma (CHA)     | Influence and the power of personality     |

| Total Roll | Attribute Score |
| :--------- | :-------------: |
| 1-4        |       -3        |
| 5-6        |       -2        |
| 7-8        |       -1        |
| 9-12       |        0        |
| 13-14      |       +1        |
| 15-16      |       +2        |
| 17-20      |       +3        |

You may swap 2 **_Attributes_** around if that would better fit your **_Character_** idea. Your **_Class_** choice will also add additional modifiers to your **_Attributes_**.

## Choose a class

Your **_Class_** represents your **_Character_**'s profession and calling - their primary role in the **_Party_**.

**_Classes_** define what your character excels at and offers different ways to solve problems and face danger, whether through steel, spells, stealth, or sacred power.

**A class will determine**

- A **_Character's_** specialized skills
- How much damage a starting **_Character_** can take (**_Hit Points_**)
- What armor and weapons they can use without additional training
- What they gain as they advance in Levels
- Starting equipment and coins

| Core Classes                | Description                                                          | Complexity (1-5) |
| :-------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------- |
| [Warrior](/classes/warrior) | A master of the arts of combat                                       | 1                |

| [Thief](/classes/thief)     | A master of stealth, acrobatics and deception                        | 2                |
