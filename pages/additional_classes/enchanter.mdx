**Starting HP :** 1d6 + ***CON***\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d6\
**Weapons & Armor :** All one-handed swords, daggers, and staves\

## Equipment
Every Enchanter starts with one of the following sets of equipment:\
A. Unseele silk cape (1 AP), fine robes, staff of white ash, silver shortsword, 4d6 coins & purse, a small log of valuable gossip.\
B. Homemade tunic, fur cloak and hood, rune carved stave, iron dagger, Enchanter's Haversack (Carry +4 extra), 2d6 coins & purse, a self-written guide to local folk beliefs.

## Spellbook

Enchanters start with a large spellbook containing a total of 1d4+2 Arcane spells from the Level 1 and 2 starting spells lists below.

## Special features

Enchanters begin with a **Familiar** that can take the shape of a small mundane animal. The Enchanter can share the senses of their familiar as well as communicate with them telepathically. 

Enchanters can cast **Hedgemagic** at will, creating a harmless magical effect.

Enchanters roll with Advantage when testing ***WIS*** to avoid damage or effects from illusions or charms.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if attributes increase, roll twice for ***INT*** *or* ***WIS***.

## Daily arcane spells

Enchanters have a number of ***Spell Slots*** they can cast each day. These represent a Enchanter's 'energy' and the taxing nature of casting spells over a long period. When they run out of spell slots, they cannot cast any more spells. See the rules for [Spellcasting](/alternate_magic).

After roughly 8 hours rest, the number of ***Spell Slots*** a ***Character*** has refreshes to its maximum.
