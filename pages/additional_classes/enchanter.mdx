## Equipment
Every Enchanter starts with one of the following sets of equipment:\
A. Unseele silk cape (1 AP), fine robes, wand of white ash (+1 SD), silver shortsword, 4d6 coins & purse, a small log of valuable gossip.\
B. Homemade tunic, fur cloak and hood, rune carved stave (+2 SD, *bulky*), iron dagger, Enchanter's Haversack (Carry +4 extra), 2d6 coins & purse, a self-written guide to local folk beliefs.

## Arcane Magic

Enchanters through training and contact with unseen powers are able to harness ***Arcane Magic***. Enchanters have ***Spell Die*** equal to their ***INT***. See rules for ***[Magic](/magic)*** and ***[Arcane Magic](/magic/arcane)***.

## Special features

Enchanters begin with a **Familiar** that can take the shape of a small mundane animal. The Enchanter can share the senses of their familiar as well as communicate with them telepathically. 

Enchanters roll with ***Advantage*** when testing ***WIS*** to avoid damage or effects from illusions or charms.

Enchanters can cast **Hedgemagic** at will, creating a harmless magical effect.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if attributes increase, roll twice for ***INT*** *or* ***WIS***.
