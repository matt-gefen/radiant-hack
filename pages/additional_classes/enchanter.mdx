**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d8\

## Equipment

Every Enchanter starts with 3d6 coins and one of the following sets of equipment:\
A. Unseele silk cape (1 AP), fine robes, wand of white ash (+1 MD), silver shortsword, a small log of valuable gossip.\
B. Homemade tunic, fur cloak and hood, rune carved stave (+2 MD, two-handed), iron dagger, Enchanter's Haversack (Carry +4 extra), a self-written guide to local folk beliefs.

## Mage

Enchanters through training and contact with unseen powers are able to harness **_Arcane Magic_**. Enchanters start with 2 **_Magic Die_** and use **_INT_** as their **_Spellcasting Attribute_**. See rules for **_[Magic](/magic)_** and **_[Arcane Magic](/magic/arcane)_**.

## Additional Skills

**Herbalist :** You know which plants and herbs can heal or produce interesting effects.
**Familiar :** You begin with a **Familiar** that can take the shape of a small mundane animal. You can share the senses of your familiar as well as communicate with them telepathically.
**Illusionist :** You roll with **_Advantage_** when testing **_WIS_** to avoid damage or effects from illusions or charms.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if attributes increase, roll twice for **_INT_** _or_ **_WIS_**.
