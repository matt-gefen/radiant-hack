# Paladin

> You are a divine warrior. You have made an binding Oath to a Higher Power and are able to call upon Miracles to do their will.

**Starting HP :** 1d10 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d10\
**Weapons & Armor :** Any and all\

## Equipment

Every Paladin starts with a **_Focus_** (_petty_) and one of the following sets of equipment:\
A - Ring mail and leather armor (2 AP), partisan (spear), a painted heater shield (1 AP), 2d8 coins and purse, censer and incense Ud8.\
B - Scale mail (3 AP), greatsword (two-handed, _Bulky_), pouch with 2d10 coins, hiking boots with wool lining, lantern and oil can Ud6.

## Oathbound

Paladins through their devotion to a **_Higher Power_** are granted the ability to channel **_Divine Magic_** and call upon **_Miracles_**. Paladins have **_Magic Die_** equal to their **_WIS_** (their **_Spellcasting Attribute_**). See rules for **_[Magic](/magic)_** and **_[Divine Magic](/magic/divine)_**.

### Prayer book

Paladins start with a **_Prayer Book_** containing the following **_Miracles_**:

| Miracle               | Description                                                                                                                                              |
| :-------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Smite**             | The caster charges their weapon with divine purpose, dealing [Sum] Bonus Damage for the next [Dice] attacks.                                             |
| **Empower**           | The caster targets [Sum] allies. For the next battle (i.e., until they’d be able to restore HP by catching their breath), they deal [Dice] Bonus Damage. |
| **Divine Protection** | The caster selects [Dice] targets, who now have 3 AP for the next [Dice] attacks they suffer.                                                            |

## Additional Skills

**_Lay on Hands :_** Once per day, cure someone of a disease or poison, or heal 1d6/Level HP.

**_Sworn Sword :_** You roll with **_Advantage_** on **_INT_** Tests to detect or search for **_Enemies of Faith_**.

**_Diplomatic :_** You have an intuitive understanding of laws and how to use them to your benefit.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if Attributes increase, roll twice for **_STR_** or **_WIS_**.
