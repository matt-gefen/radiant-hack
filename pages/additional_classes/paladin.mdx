# Paladin

> You are a divine warrior. You have made an binding Oath to a Higher Power and are able to call upon Miracles to do their will.

**Starting HP :** 1d10 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting :** 1d10\
**Weapons & Armor :** Any and All

| 2   | **Winged steel helm**, (1 AP) focus your intent to see through both magical and nonmagical darkness for Ud6 minutes. Ud8.                                                        |

## Equipment

Every Paladin starts with a **_Focus_** (_Petty_), 3d6 coins, and one of the following sets of equipment:\
A - Ring mail and leather armor (2 AP), partisan (spear), a painted heater shield (1 AP), censer and incense Ud8.\
B - Scale mail (3 AP), greatsword (two-handed, _Bulky_), hiking boots with wool lining, lantern and oil can Ud6.

## Oathbound

Paladins through an Oath to a **_Higher Power_** are granted the ability to channel **_Divine Magic_** and call upon **_Miracles_**.\
Paladins start with 1 **_Faith Die_** and use **_WIS_** as their **_Spellcasting Attribute_**.

See rules for **_[Magic](/magic)_** and **_[Divine Magic](/magic/divine)_**.

### Prayer book

Paladins start with a **_Prayer Book_** containing the following **_Miracles_**:

| Miracle               | Description                                                                                                  |
| :-------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Smite**             | The caster charges their weapon with divine purpose, dealing [Sum] Bonus Damage for the next [Dice] attacks. |
| **Divine Protection** | The caster selects [Dice] targets, who now have 3 AP for the next [Dice] attacks they suffer.                |

## Additional Skills

**_Lay on Hands :_** Once per day, cure someone of a disease or poison, or heal 1d6/**_WIS_** HP.
**_Sworn Sword :_** You roll with **_Advantage_** on **_INT_** Tests to detect or search for **_Enemies of Faith_**.
**_Diplomatic :_** You have an intuitive understanding of laws and how to use them to your benefit.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if Attributes increase, roll twice for **_STR_** or **_WIS_**.
