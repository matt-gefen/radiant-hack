# Berserker

A fighter who can harness their battle rage to vanquish their enemies.

**Starting HP :** 1d12 + **_CON_**\
**HP Per Level/Resting (HD):** 1d12 with **_Advantage_**\
**Weapons & Armor :** Any weapon, Light Armor, Medium Armors\

## Equipment

Every Berserker starts with one of the following sets of equipment:\
A. Leather Armor (2 Armor), greatsword (two-handed), 4d6 coins, a wanted poster depicting them, a distinctive scar, a horn of mead (Ud6)\
B. Hide Armor (1 Armor) and a wolf fur cloak, two hand-axes, 2d10 coins, intricate tattoos, a carved wooden mask

### Rage

Berserkers can a **_Rage_** without using an action. While in a **_Rage_** your Attacks and Damage gain **_Advantage_**. Damage dealt to you is also made with **_Advantage_**.

While in a **_Rage_**, you cannot be charmed and enemies have disadvantage on tests of Morale.

After a **_Moment_** where you do not attack/are not attacked, the **_Rage_** ends and the **_Usage Die_** is rolled.

| Level | Rage Usage Die |
| :---- | :------------- |
| 1     | Ud4            |
| 3     | Ud6            |
| 6     | Ud8            |
| 9     | Ud10           |

## Additional Skills

**Intimidate : **Test with Advantage when testing **_CHA_** for intimidating and threatening.

**Bloodlust :** Killing or Critically Damaging an enemy gains you 1d4 HP.

**Survival Expert :** You know how to live off the land. Test with **\*Advantage** on **_CON_** checks relating to wilderness survival and traversal.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if attributes increase, roll twice for **_STR_** _and_ **_CON_**.
