# Berserker

A fighter who can harness their battle rage to vanquish their enemies.

**Starting HP :** d12 + 4\
**HP Per Level/Resting (HD):** 1d12\
**Weapons & Armor :** Any and Gambeson, Leather\
**Attack Damage :** 1d8 / 1d6 Unarmed or Improvising\

## Equipment
Every Berserker starts with one of the following sets of equipment:\
A. Leather Armor (2 Armor), greatsword (two-handed weapon), 4d6 coins, a wanted poster depicting them, a distinctive scar, a horn of mead (Ud6)\
B. Improvised Hide Armor (1 Armor) and a wolf fur cloak, two hand-axes, 2d10 coins, intricate tattoos, a carved wooden mask

## Special features

Test with Advantage when testing ***CHA*** for intimidating and threatening.

### Rage
Berserkers can enter a ***Rage*** without using an action. While in a ***Rage***, their Attack Damage die upgrades by one level (d10/d8) and they gain advantage on attack and damage rolls. 

While in a ***Rage***, they cannot be charmed and enemies have disadvantage on tests of Morale.

After a ***Moment*** where the Berserker does not attack/is not attacked, the ***Rage*** ends and the ***Usage Die*** is rolled.

| Level | Rage Usage Die |
| :---- | :------------- |
| 1     | Ud4            |
| 3     | Ud6            |
| 6     | Ud8            |
| 9     | Ud10           |

## Leveling up

Roll to see if attributes increase, roll twice for ***STR*** *and* ***CON***.

Gain 1HD - Roll 1d12 with Advantage to gain that many additional maximum HP.

At Levels 3, 6, and 9 the ***Rage*** usage dice improves (see above table)
