## Equipment

Every Berserker starts with 3d6 coins and one of the following sets of equipment:\
A. Leather Armor (2 Armor), greatsword (two-handed), a wanted poster depicting them, a distinctive scar, a horn of mead (Ud6)\
B. Hide Armor (1 Armor) and a wolf fur cloak, two hand-axes, intricate tattoos, a carved wooden mask

## Martialist

You move through combat like a bloody storm. Start with 1 **_Grit_** (Ud6) and knowledge of the following **_Techniques_**

Roll **_Grit_** to use these techniques with [Dice] being the amount of Dice rolled and [Sum] equaling the total number rolled.

| Technique       | Description                                                                                                                                |
| :-------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Rage :**      | Enter a state of Rage for **_STR_** Moments, rolling any damage with **_Advantage_**. All incoming damage is rolled at **_Disadvantage_**. |
| **Daunt :**     | When you next deal damage, force that target to make a Morale Test.                                                                        |
| **Bloodlust :** | For [Dice]+1 Moments, gain [Sum] HP when dealing damage.                                                                                   |

## Additional Skills

**Intimidate :** Your presence can be frightening to some. Test with **_Advantage_** when testing **_CHA_** for intimidating and threatening.
**Survival Expert :** You know how to live off the land. Test with **_Advantage_** on **_CON_** checks relating to wilderness survival and traversal.
**Sturdy :** Roll with **_Advantage_** when testing **_CON_** to avoid damage from poison or being **_Paralyzed_**.

## Leveling up

Roll to see if attributes increase, roll twice for **_STR_** _and_ **_CON_**.
