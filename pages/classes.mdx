# Classes

> Your **_Class_** represents your **_Character_**'s profession and calling - their primary role in the **_Party_**.

1. [Warrior](/classes/warrior) - A master of the arts of combat
2. [Rogue](/classes/rogue) - An agile and persuasive problem solver
3. [Mage](/classes/mage) - A magical adept, casting spells with incredible skill 
4. [Cleric](/classes/cleric) - A channeler of a higher power that can turn belief into powerful magic
