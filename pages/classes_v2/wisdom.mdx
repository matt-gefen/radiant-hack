# Knowledge
*Mastermind*
- **SENTINEL**: You are incredibly difficult to surprise. Take +1d to resist consequences from being surprised. If there is a question as to who acts first in combat, it is you
- **PLANS WITHIN PLANS**: Lower cost, higher effect flashbacks when described as a plan falling into place
- **BEND CHANCE**: Spend X momentum to re-roll a check
- **STOLEN TIME**: negate rolls from enemies
- **OBSERVE THE THREADS OF FATE**: Divine for increased potency or lower risk on an action
