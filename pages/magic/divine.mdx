# Divine Magic
Divine magic is a bit more rigid as opposed to the more free-form arcane magic.

## Higher Powers
To cast ***Miracles***, one needs to make a vow to a ***Higher Power***. This ***Higher Power*** can be a god, a concept (vengeance, chaos), or an abstract construct (nature).

***Higher Powers*** tend to have one or two domains, such as courage, death, darkness, earth, life, fire, balance, glory, joy, inspiration, music, the sea, shadows, or animals. The [Cleric]("/classes/cleric") Class has some examples of ***Higher Powers*** written into their starting relics.

A ***Higher Power*** also has ***Enemies of Faith***: those deemed abhorrent, heretical or “evil”, such as the unjust and criminal, the greedy, demons, the undead, abominations, dragons, or those harming nature.

They also have a preferred form of ***Worship***: specific rituals, acts, prayers, and frequency. 

**Prayer**
During a ***Respite***, say a short prayer to your ***Higher Power***

Determine how pious your day was, together with the GM. Depending on the content of your prayer and the actions during the day, the GM may assign a bonus ranging from 0 to +3 to the Character's ***Spell Attribute*** for the following day.

## Miracles
***Spell Dice*** can be used to cast known ***Miracles***. ***Miracles*** can reach targets up to Far.

Miracles are kept in a ***Prayer Book*** (or a similar item), which takes up 1 inventory slot. If this book is lost, the devotee cannot change their currently imprinted ***Miracles***. 

For the "complete" list of Miracles, ***[check here](/magic/spells/miracles)***

### Casting Miracles
You have a number of ***Spell Dice (SD)*** (d6) depending on your ***Class***. Choose how many you wish to invest.

Roll the invested ***Spell Dice***. For each die that shows a 1-3, you take 1 Damage. For each die that shows 4-6, you take 2 Damage. Your Miracle will also use the result of this roll; [Dice] stands for the total number of dice rolled, [Sum] for the total sum of the values. Furthermore, Bonus Damage mentioned in some miracles cannot exceed the maximum natural roll possible (i.e. with Bonus Damage +2, a roll of 4 on a d6 is raised to 6, a 5 is raised to 6, and it has no effect on a 6). It also does not enhance magical damage.

Only ***Miracles*** which are imprinted into the mind of the devotee can be cast. An imprinted Miracle takes up 1 inventory slot. This represents the mental burden of having an elaborate prayer memorized. Imprinted ***Miracles*** can be swapped, added, and removed every ***Respite***. 

It can also be done during an adventure, in which case it requires 10 minutes and a DR12 ***Spell Attribute*** Test. The desired changes take effect regardless of the outcome, but on a failure, the devotee cannot change ***Miracles*** again until sunrise. 

Imprinted Miracles cannot be “dropped” at will, but they can be forcibly “forgotten” due to severe damage.

#### Focus
Casting a ***Miracle*** requires having one hand free to hold on to a ***Focus***. This can be a ring, a necklace, a bracelet with prayer beads, sacred tattoos, or another type of trinket, and it does not take up an inventory slot. Many gods are petty and require a devotee to carry their ***Focus*** out in the open, identifiable by all. Hiding one's divine allegiance is frowned upon by ***Higher Powers***.
