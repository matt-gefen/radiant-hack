# Divine Magic

**_Divine Magic_** is a bit more rigid as opposed to the more free-form **_Arcane Magic_** but as a result, is far less risky. There is no chance of **_Arcane Feedback_**.

## Higher Powers

To cast **_Divine Magic_**, one needs to have made been granted the ability through devotion or oaths to a **_Higher Power_**. This **_Higher Power_** can be a god, a concept (vengeance, chaos), or an abstract construct (nature).

**_Higher Powers_** tend to have one or two domains, such as courage, death, darkness, earth, life, fire, balance, glory, joy, inspiration, music, the sea, shadows, or animals. The [Cleric]("/classes/cleric") Class has some examples of **_Higher Powers_** written into their starting items.

A **_Higher Power_** also has **_Enemies of Faith_**: those deemed abhorrent, heretical or “evil”, such as the unjust and criminal, the greedy, demons, the undead, abominations, dragons, or those harming nature.

They also have a preferred form of **_Worship_**: specific rituals, acts, prayers, and frequency.

### Prayer

During a **_Respite_**, say a short prayer to your **_Higher Power_**

Determine how pious your **_Character_** was since their last **_Respite_**, together with the GM. Depending on the content of your prayer and the actions during this time, the **_GM_** may assign a bonus ranging from 0 to +3 to the Character's **_Spell Attribute_** for the following day.

## Miracles

**_Faith Dice_** can be used to cast known **_Miracles_**. **_Miracles_** can reach targets up to **_Far_**.

For the "complete" list of Miracles, **_[check here](/magic/spells/miracles)_**

#### Focus

Casting a **_Miracle_** requires having a **_Focus_**. For **_Divine Magic_**, This can be a ring, a necklace, a bracelet with prayer beads, sacred tattoos, or another type of trinket, and it does not take up an inventory slot. Many gods are petty and require a devotee to carry their **_Focus_** out in the open, identifiable by all. Hiding one's divine allegiance is frowned upon by **_Higher Powers_**.
