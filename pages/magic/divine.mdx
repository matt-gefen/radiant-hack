# Divine Magic

**_Divine Magic_** is a bit more rigid as opposed to the more free-form **_Arcane Magic_** but as a result, is far less risky.

## Higher Powers

To cast **_Miracles_**, one needs to make a vow to a **_Higher Power_**. This **_Higher Power_** can be a god, a concept (vengeance, chaos), or an abstract construct (nature).

**_Higher Powers_** tend to have one or two domains, such as courage, death, darkness, earth, life, fire, balance, glory, joy, inspiration, music, the sea, shadows, or animals. The [Cleric]("/classes/cleric") Class has some examples of **_Higher Powers_** written into their starting items.

A **_Higher Power_** also has **_Enemies of Faith_**: those deemed abhorrent, heretical or “evil”, such as the unjust and criminal, the greedy, demons, the undead, abominations, dragons, or those harming nature.

They also have a preferred form of **_Worship_**: specific rituals, acts, prayers, and frequency.

**Prayer**

During a **_Respite_**, say a short prayer to your **_Higher Power_**

Determine how pious your **_Character_** was since their last Rest, together with the GM. Depending on the content of your prayer and the actions during this time, the **_GM_** may assign a bonus ranging from 0 to +3 to the Character's **_Spell Attribute_** for the following day.

## Miracles

**_Spell Dice_** can be used to cast known **_Miracles_**. **_Miracles_** can reach targets up to Far.

Miracles are kept in a **_Prayer Book_** (or a similar item), which takes up 1 inventory slot. If this book is lost, the devotee cannot change their currently imprinted **_Miracles_**.

Only **_Miracles_** which are imprinted into the mind of the devotee can be cast. An imprinted Miracle takes up 1 inventory slot. This represents the mental burden of having an elaborate prayer memorized. Imprinted **_Miracles_** can be swapped, added, and removed every **_Respite_**.

It can also be done during an adventure, in which case it requires 10 minutes and a DR12 **_Spell Attribute_** Test. The desired changes take effect regardless of the outcome, but on a failure, the devotee cannot change **_Miracles_** again until sunrise.

Imprinted **_Miracles_** cannot be “dropped” at will, but they can be forcibly “forgotten” due to severe damage.

For the "complete" list of Miracles, **_[check here](/magic/spells/miracles)_**

### Casting Miracles

You have a number of **_Spell Dice (SD)_** (d6) depending on your **_Class_**. Choose how many you wish to invest.

Roll the invested **_Spell Dice_**. Your **_Miracle_** will use the result of this roll: [Dice] stands for the total number of dice rolled, [Sum] for the total sum of the values. Furthermore, **_Bonus Damage_** mentioned in some miracles cannot exceed the maximum natural roll possible (i.e. with Bonus Damage +2, a roll of 4 on a d6 is raised to 6, a 5 is raised to 6, and it has no effect on a 6). It also does not enhance magical damage.

Play out the effect of the chosen **_Miracle_**

Test the **_Character's_** **_Spell Attribute_** - adding the number of invested **_Spell Dice_** to a DR12. If they fail then they reduce the number of **_Spell Dice_** they have available by 1.

#### Focus

Casting a **_Miracle_** requires having one hand free to hold on to a **_Focus_**. This can be a ring, a necklace, a bracelet with prayer beads, sacred tattoos, or another type of trinket, and it does not take up an inventory slot. Many gods are petty and require a devotee to carry their **_Focus_** out in the open, identifiable by all. Hiding one's divine allegiance is frowned upon by **_Higher Powers_**.
