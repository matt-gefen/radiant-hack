# Divine prayers

Classes that can learn Divine prayers include the [Cleric](/classes/cleric.mdx) and the [Paladin](/additional_classes/paladin.mdx)

> Note for development and spell revision: \
> If a spell from a traditional OSR or original era game calls for a creature to make a save, the character must test their INT or WIS - to see if the magic cast was powerful enough to overcome their defences (remember the [Powerful Opponents](/rules#powerful-opponents) rule).

## Level 1
* **Bless Water :** Transmute a flask of water into holy water.
* **Cure Light Wounds :** Heal 1d8 HP to a Nearby target.
* **Detect Evil & Undeath :** Everything Nearby that is evil or undead glows - lasts Ud6 Minutes.
* **Heroism :** Nearby target gains advantage on all tests and +1d4 temporary HP. Ud4 Moments.
* **Light :** Create dim light from a Nearby spot or object - Ud8 Minutes.
* **Protection from Evil :** Advantage on all harmful tests from an Evil source - Ud8 Minutes.
* **Purify Food and Drink :** Removes all diseases from all Nearby food and drink.
* **Protection from Evil :** Advantage on all harmful tests against an evil source - lasts Ud8 Minutes.
* **Sanctuary :** Nearby Target can't attack or cast offensive effects. Attackers must pass WIS test to target them. Ends if target attacks.
* **Resist Element :** Nearby target gains +2 to tests defending against a chosen element (fire, frost, lightning, acid, stone, air). Ud8 Moments.
* **Ward Weapon**: Bless a Nearby weapon to deal full damage to incorporeal creatures. Ud8 Minutes.

## Level 2

* **Augery :** Learn if an action in the next hour will bring a good or negative outcome. 25% chance that the reading is innacurate. Repeated questions give disadvantage.
* **Bless :** Nearby allies gain +1 to stats when making attacks and saves - Ud8 Minutes.
* **Find Traps :** Notice all Nearby traps - lasts Ud6 Minutes.
* **Locate Object :** Name an object and know its direction if within 1000 ft.
* **Sacred Brand**: Mark a Nearby target with a holy symbol. The caster can sense their direction and distance. Ud8 Hours.
* **Silence :** Magical silence covering everything Nearby to a target - Ud8 Minutes.
* **Speak with Animals :** Can understand and talk with animals - Ud8 Minutes.
* **Spiritual Weapon :** Create a floating holy weapon that deals 1d4/level damage to a single target on your turn. Can hit incorporeal creatures. Must stay Nearby the caster. Ud8 Moments.

## Level 3

* **Daylight :** A nearby area is illuminated by sunlight - Ud8 minutes.
* **Cure Disease :** Cures a Nearby target of all diseases.
* **Hold Person**: Paralyses 1d4 Nearby targets. Test WIS each Turn to see if the effect lasts.
* **Locate Object :** Sense direction of a known object - Ud6 minutes.
* **Prayer :** All Nearby allies defend against attacks with Advantage - Ud4 moments.
* **Remove Curse :** Removes a curse from a Nearby target.
* **Remove Paralysis :**
* **Speak with Dead :** Ask a Nearby corpse 3 questions.
* **Silverlight :** Create a bright silvery light that inflicts **Weakness** to those vulnerable to silver Nearby. Ud10 Minutes.

## Level 4

* **Create Food/Water :** Create enough food/water for all Close creatures for a day.
* **Cure Serious Wounds :** Heal 3d8+3 HP to a Nearby target.
* **Divine Wrath :** Call down strikes of divine energy on 1d4 Nearby targets. Each takes 2d8 damage. Test **WIS** for all targets to blind for Ud6 Moments.
* **Neutralise Poison :** Remove poison from a Nearby target.
* **Protection:** Grants nearby characters an aura of +3 Armor temporarily. The aura lasts up to 10 minutes but ends when the character takes damage.
* **Reject Element :** * Nearby target gains immunity against a chosen element (fire, frost, lightning, acid, stone, air). Ud6 Moments.
* **Sacred Ground :** Consecrate a Nearby area. All healing prayers cast within heal +1d8 HP. Ud8 Hours.

## Level 5

* **Balefyre :** 4d6 Nearby Targets ignite with divine flames. Creatures take 1d6/level damage and ***Ongoing Damage***.
* **Commune :** The Cleric's deity truthfully answers 3 questions.
* **Dispel Evil :** Removes a Nearby evil spell.
* **Ethereal Jaunt :** Briefly phase into the ethereal plane. Move through solid objects and avoid all damage. Ud6 Moments.
* **Finger of Death :** Choose a Nearby target and test **WIS**, if a pass the target is OofA.
* **Plague :** Test WIS for 1d12 Nearby targets. On a success, they lose 2d8 HP and take Ongoing Damage.
* **Quest :** Force a Nearby Creature to obey a complex series of up to 2d4 orders or steps.
* **Raise Dead :** Return a Nearby willing target to life, who's died within the last 7 days.

## Level 6

* **Animate Object :** Give a Nearby object motion and a simple intelligence.
* **Banishment :** Force a Nearby extraplanar creature back to their home plane. If native to this plane, they are sent to a harmless demiplane for Ud10 Minutes. WIS save negates this prayer.
* **Blade Barrier :** Blades cover a Nearby area, any Creature that comes Close takes its HD in damage.
* **Conjure Elemental :** Summons an elemental with HD equal to caster's lvl - Ud12 minutes.
* **Death Ward :** Nearby target is immune to death spells.
* **Champion's Aura :** Gain advantage on all attacks and deal +1d6 holy damage. Ud8 Moments.
* **Find Path :** The path to a chosen location is made known - Ud10 minutes.
* **Hallowed Ground :** Permanently consecrate a Nearby area. Undead cannot enter and all healing prayers cast within heal +1d8 HP.
* **Mass Healing :** All Nearby allies heal 4d8+4 HP and are cured of one condition.
* **True Seeing :** See through darkness, illusions, invisibility, and shapeshifting. See ethereal creatures. Ud12 Hours.
* **Word of Recall :** Gives the caster the ability to teleport back to the location this spell was cast.

## Level 7

* **Aerial Servant :** Summons an elemental servant to recover a distant object.
* **Astral Spell :** Projects an avatar of the caster onto a chosen plane or to a chosen place - Ud8 minutes.
* **Balefyre Judgement :** One target bursts into divine flames, taking 2d6/level damage and Ongoing Damage. Target also becomes a source of Balefyre, dealing 1d6 damage to Nearby creatures each round. Ud12 Rounds.
* **Divine Vessel :** Nearby target becomes a vessel of divine power. Target tests WIS to blinds evil creatures in Close range for Ud6 Minutes. Target also gets +4 Armor and advantage when defending from spells.
* **Control Weather :** Control the Nearby weather to all extremes - Ud6 minutes.
* **Earthquake :** Test **WIS** for all Nearby creatures, passes are taken OofA.
* **Gate :** Open a portal to another plane or summon a powerful being chosen by your diety. Ud20 Hours.
* **Holy Word :** Nearby Creatures with 5HD or less drop dead, those with 6-8HD are Paralysed and Creatures with 9-10HD cannot make an Action for the next 1d6 Minutes.
* **Wind Walk :** Turn into mist and back, at will for the rest of the session.
* **Restoration :** Returns all levels lost by the caster or a single Nearby Creature with level drain.
