# Divine Miracles

> Miracles are granted to followers of Higher Powers through devotion

| Miracle                                                 | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| :------------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <div id="smite">**Smite**</div>                         | The caster charges their weapon with divine purpose, dealing [Sum] Bonus Damage for the next [Dice] attacks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| <div id="empower">**Empower**</div>                     | The caster targets [Sum] allies. For the next battle (i.e., until they’d be able to restore HP by catching their breath), they deal [Dice] Bonus Damage.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| <div id="divine_protection">**Divine Protection**</div> | The caster selects [Dice] targets, who now have 3 AP for the next [Dice] attacks they suffer.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| <div id="rebuke">**Rebuke**</div>                       | The caster targets [Sum] **_Enemies of Faith_**. For each, the caster must make a DR12 - [Dice] WIS Test. On a success, they will flee in fear.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| <div id="bless">**Bless**</div>                         | The caster declares the allies they want to bless and rolls MD. The total value of [Sum] becomes a Pool of points that those blessed can tap into at will when making Tests. The Pool lasts until they next **_Rest_**. The moment an ally needs to roll a Test, they can use up a number of points from the Pool to temporarily raise a single Attribute. The Pool must be tapped into before rolling the Test. |
| <div id="replenish">**Replenish**</div>                 | The caster targets [Dice] allies, who regain [Sum] HP each, not exceeding their maximum HP. This means that a roll of 2 MD, with results of 4 and 6, allows 2 allies to regain 10 HP each.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
