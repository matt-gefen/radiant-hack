# Arcane Magic

## Casting Magic

Arcane magic requires **_Spell Dice (SD)_** (d6). **_Spell Dice_** can be gained through a **_Character's_** **_Class_** or worn Equipment.

Roll the invested **_Spell Dice_**. For each 6, you can choose to roll an additional SD, even beyond your skills or gear. The total rolled SD can never exceed 4.

Play out the effects of the chosen spell.

Test the **_Character's_** **_Spell Attribute_** - adding the number of invested **_Spell Dice_** to a DR12. If they fail then they reduce the number of **_Spell Dice_** they have available by 1. The **_Spell Dice_** consumed first are those given by equipment.

Note any duplicate values. This indicates something has gone very wrong. The sum of the dice that show duplicate values are dealt as damage in the form of **_Arcane Feedback_**.

For instance, rolling 4-3-3 means taking 6 damage. If damage taken is larger than 6, it has the **Blast** property, affecting anyone **_Close_**. **_Arcane Feedback_** occurs after a spell has taken effect.

## Arcane Equipment

- **Trinkets** (bracelets, rings, necklaces, circlets, etc.) give additional SD when worn. These SD do not stack unless otherwise noted.
- **Wands** can be held in one's hand to grant +1-2 SD.
- **Staff/Staves** (two-handed) grant +2-4 SD when wielded and can double as a weapon.

In addition to simply granting additional **_Spell Dice_**, wand and staff SD can be used to can negate **_Spell Dice_**. This negation can be used on **_Arcane Feedback_** or an enemy spell. When SD is depleted, the item becomes _cracked_ and does not grant SD until repaired during a **_[Respite](/rules#respite)_**. When using **_Power_** to counter an enemy's spell, the wielder declares the number of dice they intend to negate after they know how many dice are rolled, but before they know the outcome.

**Example Arcane Equipment:** _Gnarled Oaken Staff_ (+2 SD)

## Types of Spells

1. **_Sculpt Energy_** to cast damaging spells in a wide variety of variations
2. **_Tricks_** are spells that hinder but do not damage opponents
3. **_Counterspell_** is a form of defense against magic
4. **_Practical Magic_** are all other magic effects: those affecting the environment and allies

### Sculpt Energy

Any arcane magic user can cast a bolt of arcane energy. This energy can be sculpted to the spellcaster's will. The amount of modification depends on the number of SD rolled.

It costs 1 SD to cast a basic **Arcane Bolt**, as seen in the table below. For each extra SD rolled, the caster can pick one property to enhance.

| Spell Power | 1MD             | +1MD                                          | +2MD              | +3MD      |
| :---------- | :-------------- | :-------------------------------------------- | :---------------- | :-------- |
| Damage      | 1d6/level       | 2d6/level                                     | 3d6/level         | 4d6/level |
| Range       | Near            | Far                                           | Distant           |           |
| Speed       | Full Turn       | Action                                        |                   |           |
| Targets     | 1               | 2                                             | Blast (all Close) |           |
| Elements    | None            | 1 (Fire, Ice, Shock, etc.)                    |                   |           |
| Damage Type | Physical Damage | Hinderance (Weakened, Stuck, Paralyzed, etc.) | Ongoing Damage    |           |

**_Players_** are encouraged to be creative in their energy-sculpting; **_Players_** can simply describe what they would like to happen with their spell, and the **_GM_** can indicate how many SD that might take. The general rule of thumb is that SD can be used to add a damage die or interesting effect at a 1:1 rate.

### Tricks

**_Tricks_** never deal damage, are always single **_Actions_**, and have a maximum range of **_Near_**. **_Tricks_** can be used to stun, shove, disarm, trip, etc.

When performing a **_Trick_**:

- The magic user describes the desired effect
- The Warden will decide on a fitting **_Test_** for the **_Character_**

Just like with **_Sculpt Energy_**, SD can be used to enhance **_Tricks_**. Casting a **_Trick_** at all costs 1 SD, which can be enhanced with:

- **Difficulty :** For each SD, raise the DR of the Test by 2 (default is DR12).
- **Targets :** the Trick affects 1 additional foe Near the main target for each SD invested.

#### Quick Example

The Wizard tries to make an enemy orc stumble and invests 2 SD to cast a **_Trick_**. She rolls a 3 and a 5. 1 SD is used to cast the **_Trick_**, and 1 SD is used to increase the difficulty. The orc must succeed on a DR12 Test, but due to the difficulty increase, the DR is raised by 2 to DR14 for this Test. The Wizard then must succeed at a **_INT_** test with a DR14 (12 + 2SD) or expend a **_Spell Die_**.

### Counterspell

Anyone with **_Spell Dice_** at their disposal can, at the moment an enemy spell is cast, attempt a **_Counterspell_**

This option is not available if the caster is already focusing on a spell themselves. A **_Counterspell_** simply means investing and rolling an available number of SD, following the same rules for casting. If the total roll is equal to or higher than the total roll of the enemy spell, the two cancel each other out in a bright display of energy and light. If the roll is lower than the enemy spell, the counterspell fails and the enemy spell takes effect as normal.

### Practical Magic

Any magic that doesn’t do direct damage (**_Sculpt Energy_**) or indirect harm (**_Tricks_**) can be improvised free-form by simply determining the SD value.

- 1 SD: A small, personal effect. Could be achieved with tools instead of magic (lighting a torch, growing vines as a rope).
- 2 SD: An interpersonal or room-sized effect. Barely beyond the possibility of tools (a small wall of force, breathing underwater, camouflage, sending telepathic messages).
- 3 SD: Large, “impossible” effects. Makes a mockery of the normal laws of nature (animating the dead or inanimate objects, teleportation).
- 4 SD: Mythical, highly dangerous alterations to reality itself.

**_Practical Magic_** is designed to be very free-form. However, the list of [Arcane Words of Power](/arcane_words_of_power) can serve as inspiration for what kinds of spells are possible.

## The Power of Magic

A spellcaster with 4 SD can use their **_Sculpt Energy_** to deal 4d6 damage, no questions asked. How is this fair?. It isn’t. A 4 SD arcane spellcaster is a magical nuke. This also makes them primary targets.

4 SD has considerable risk of **_Arcane Feedback_**. 2 SD has a chance of 16%, 3 SD has a chance of 44%, 4 SD has a chance of 72%!
