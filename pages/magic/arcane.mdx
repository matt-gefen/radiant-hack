# Arcane Magic

## Casting Magic
Arcane magic requires ***Spell Dice (SD)*** (d6). ***Spell Dice*** can be gained through a Character's ***Class*** or worn Equipment.

Roll the invested ***Spell Dice***. For each 6, you can choose to roll an additional SD, even beyond your skills or gear. The total rolled SD can never exceed 4.

Play out the effects of the chosen spell.

Test the ***Character's*** ***Spell Attribute*** - adding the number of invested ***Spell Dice*** to a DR12. If they fail then they reduce the number of ***Spell Dice*** they have available by 1. The ***Spell Dice*** consumed first are those given by equipment.

Note any duplicate values. This indicates something has gone very wrong. The sum of the dice that show duplicate values are dealt as damage to HP, then to WIL, in the form of ***Arcane Feedback***.

For instance, rolling 4-3-3 means taking 6 damage. If damage taken is larger than 6, it has the **Blast** property, affecting anyone ***Close***. ***Arcane Feedback*** occurs after a spell has taken effect.

## Arcane Equipment
- **Trinkets** (bracelets, rings, necklaces, circlets, etc.) give additional SD when worn.
- **Wands** can be held in one's hand to grant +1-2 SD.
- **Staff/Staves** (two-handed) grant +2-4 SD when wielded and can double as a weapon.

In addition to simply granting additional ***Spell Dice***, wand and staff SD can be used to can negate ***Spell Dice***. This negation can be used on ***Arcane Feedback*** or an enemy spell. When SD is depleted, the item becomes cracked and does not grant SD until repaired during a ***[Respite](/rules#respite)***. When using ***Power*** to counter an enemy's spell, the wielder declares the number of dice they intend to negate after they know how many dice are rolled, but before they know the outcome.

**Example:** *Gnarled Oaken Staff* (+2 SD)

## Types of Spells
1. ***Sculpt Energy*** to cast damaging spells in a wide variety of variations
2. ***Tricks*** are spells that hinder but do not damage opponents
3. ***Counterspell*** is a form of defense against magic
4. ***Practical Magic*** are all other magic effects: those affecting the environment and allies

### Sculpt Energy
Any magic user can cast a bolt of arcane energy. This energy can be sculpted to the spellcaster's will. The amount of modification depends on the number of SD rolled.

It costs 1 SD to cast a basic **Arcane Bolt**, as seen in the table below. For each extra SD rolled, the caster can pick one property to enhance.

| Spell Power | 1MD             | +1MD                                          | +2MD              | +3MD      |
| :---------- | :-------------- | :-------------------------------------------- | :---------------- | :-------- |
| Damage      | 1d6/level       | 2d6/level                                     | 3d6/level         | 4d6/level |
| Range       | Near            | Far                                           | Distant           |           |
| Speed       | Full Turn       | Action                                        |                   |           |
| Targets     | 1               | 2                                             | Blast (all Close) |           |
| Elements    | None            | 1 (Fire, Ice, Shock, etc.)                    |                   |           |
| Damage Type | Physical Damage | Hinderance (Weakened, Stuck, Paralyzed, etc.) | Ongoing Damage    |           |

Players are encouraged to be creative in their energy-sculpting; a player can simply describe what they would like to happen with their spell, and the GM can indicate how many SD that might take. The general rule of thumb is that SD can be used to add a damage die or interesting effect at a 1:1 rate.

### Tricks
***Tricks*** never deal damage, are always single ***Actions***, and have a maximum range of ***Near***. ***Tricks*** can be used to stun, shove, disarm, trip, etc.

When performing a ***Trick***:
- The magic user describes the desired effect
- The Warden will decide on a fitting ***Test*** for the target

Just like with ***Sculpt Energy***, SD can be used to enhance ***Tricks***. Casting a ***Trick*** at all costs 1 SD, which can be enhanced with:
- **Difficulty :** For each SD, raise the DR of the Test by 2 (default is DR12).
- **Targets :** the Trick affects 1 additional foe Near the main target for each SD invested.

#### Quick Example
The Wizard tries to make an enemy orc stumble and invests 2 SD to cast a ***Trick***. He rolls a 3 and a 5. 1 SD is used to cast the ***Trick***, and 1 SD is used to increase the difficulty. The orc must succeed on a DR12 Test, but due to the difficulty increase, the DR is raised by 2 to DR14 for this Test. The Wizard then must succeed at a ***INT*** test with a DR14 (12 + 2SD) or expend a ***Spell Die***.

### Counterspell
Anyone with ***Spell Dice*** at their disposal can, at the moment an enemy spell is cast, attempt a ***Counterspell***

This option is not available if the caster is already focusing on a spell themselves. A ***Counterspell*** simply means investing and rolling an available number of SD, following the same rules for casting. If the total roll is equal to or higher than the total roll of the enemy spell, the two cancel each other out in a bright display of energy and light. If the roll is lower than the enemy spell, the counterspell fails and the enemy spell takes effect as normal.

### Practical Magic
Any magic that doesn’t do direct damage (***Sculpt Energy***) or indirect harm (***Tricks***) can be improvised free-form by simply determining the SD value.

- 1 SD: A small, personal effect. Could be achieved with tools instead of magic (lighting a torch, growing vines as a rope).
- 2 SD: An interpersonal or room-sized effect. Barely beyond the possibility of tools (a small wall of force, breathing underwater, camouflage, sending telepathic messages).
- 3 SD: Large, “impossible” effects. Makes a mockery of the normal laws of nature (animating the dead or inanimate objects, teleportation).
- 4 SD: Mythical, highly dangerous alterations to reality itself.

***Practical Magic*** is designed to be very free-form. However, the list of [Arcane Words of Power](/arcane_words_of_power) can serve as inspiration for what kinds of spells are possible.

## The Power of Magic

A Wizard with 4 SD can use their ***Sculpt Energy*** to deal 4d6 damage, no questions asked. How is this fair?. It isn’t. A 4 SD wizard is a magical nuke. This also makes them primary targets.

4 SD has considerable risk of Arcane Feedback. 2 SD has a chance of 16%, 3 SD has a chance of 44%, 4 SD has a chance of 72%!
