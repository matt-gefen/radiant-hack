# Health and Harm

## Health

Every **_Character_** and **_Creature_** has something called a **_Hit Points_** (HP). This number represents the amount of damage/injury they can withstand before going **_OofA_**.
Characters also have **_Hit Dice_** (HD) determined by their Class that indicate how many dice to roll to determine the number of **_Hit Points_** they begin the game with.

**_Characters_** can gain **_Hit Points_** from Spells, Potions, Items, and Class Features. They can never gain more than their maximum - and can never go below zero either.\
When healing a **_Character_** who is [**_Out of Action (OofA)_**](#death-and-dying), just start at zero and count up. That **_Character_** is now back on their feet and no longer **_OofA_**.

## Resting

Characters can take some time to tend to their wounds.

### Breather

When **_Characters_** rest for about an hour, they...

- Can roll a **_Hit Die_** and regain that much HP if you have food/rations to eat. Mark a use of that food.
- Can attempt to repair gear with a **_STR_** Test.
- Attempt to tend addititonal wounds with an **_INT_** Test. Success restores an additional 1d6 HP. Another **_Character_** may make the **_INT_** Test, but they can only treat one wounded during a **_Breather_**.

### Respite

After roughly 8 hours of rest...

- Characters regain all of their HP. If Characters do not take the time to hunt or forage first, they mark a use of food. If they do not have food, they do not regain HP.
- Regain use of all relevant abilities.
- Repair gear.

### Deprivation

A **_Character_** with unmet critical needs (food, rest) after a full day become **_Deprived_**. Someone who is **_Deprived_** is **Weakened** and no longer gains HP by taking a **_Breather_**. After three days with this status, the **_Character_** will fall unconscious and die of exhaustion a day later.

## Death and dying

When a **_Character_** is reduced to zero **_Hit Points (HP)_** they are taken **_Out of Action (OofA)_**, they are unconscious and cannot make any actions. When the fight is over/are out of danger, a **_Character_** that is taken **_OofA_** can roll on the table below to see what happens to them. **If they survive they gain 1d4 HP**.

> If the **_Characters_** lose the fight or are unable to recover the body of the character, they are lost forever!

| OUT | OF Action                                                           |
| :-: | :------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|  1  | **KO’d** - Just knocked out.                                        |
|  2  | **Fat Head** - Disadvantage on all tests for the next hour.         |
|  3  | **Cracked Bones** - STR, DEX and CON are temp. -2 for the next day. |
|  4  | **Crippled** - STR or DEX is permanently reduced by 2               |
|  5  | **Disfigured** - CHA is permanently reduced by 2.                   |
|  6  | **Dead** - Not alive anymore.                                       |
