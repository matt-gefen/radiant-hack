# Magic

Magic is split up into two main variants:

1. [Arcane Magic](/magic/arcane) taps into a nearly limitless well of power but comes with significant risk.
2. [Divine Magic](magic/divine) allows for the channeling of powerful but more structured Miracles granted from oaths to Higher Powers.

The methods of spellcasting differ between these two types.

## Terms

[Dice] or MD are equal to **_Magic Dice_** invested in the spell.\
[Sum] is a number equal to the sum of rolled **_Magic Dice_**.

## Spellcasting

Those who have the skills to cast spells innately have a number of **_Magic Dice_** equal to their **_Spell Attribute_** they can cast each day. These represent a magic user's 'energy' and the taxing nature of casting spells over a long period. When they run out of **_Magic Dice_**, they cannot cast any more spells. Some equipment grants additional **_Magic Dice_**.

To cast a spell, the spellcaster must test their ***Spell Attribute*** at DR12 + [Dice] If they pass, the spell is successfully cast. On a 1, roll on the Magical Mishaps table. On a 20, no MD will be consumed by the spell nor is there any chance of **_Arcane Feedback_** (for **_Arcane Magic_**).

For each 6, you can choose to roll an additional MD, even beyond your skills or gear. The total rolled MD can never exceed 4.

Play out the effects of the chosen spell.

For every 4-6 rolled they reduce the number of Magic Dice they have available by 1. The Magic Dice consumed first are those given by equipment.

After roughly 8 hours rest (**_[Respite](#resting)_**), the number of **_Magic Dice_** a **_Character_** has refreshes to its maximum.
