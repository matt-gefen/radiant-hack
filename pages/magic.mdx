# Magic
Magic is split up into two main variants:
1. [Arcane Magic]('/magic/arcane') hurts and hinders opponents
2. [Divine Magic]('magic/divine') heals and enhances allies

The methods of spellcasting differ between these two types and use different resources

**Terms:**
[Dice] are equal to Magic/Faith Dice invested in the spell.
[Sum] is a number equal to the sum of rolled Magic/Faith Dice.
