# Magic
Magic is split up into two main variants:
1. [Arcane Magic]('/magic/arcane') taps into a limitless well and is capable of great power but comes with significant risk.
2. [Divine Magic]('magic/divine') allows for the channeling of powerful but restricted Miracles granted from oaths to Higher Powers.

The methods of spellcasting differ between these two types.

**Terms:**\
[Dice] or SD are equal to Spell Dice invested in the spell.\
[Sum] is a number equal to the sum of rolled Spell Dice.
