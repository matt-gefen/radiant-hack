# Magic

Magic is split up into two main variants:

1. [Arcane Magic](/magic/arcane) taps into a nearly limitless well of power but comes with significant risk.
2. [Divine Magic](magic/divine) allows for the channeling of powerful but more structured Miracles granted from oaths to Higher Powers.

The methods of spellcasting differ between these two types.

## Terms

[Dice] is equal to the number of **_Magic Dice_** (MD) or **_Faith Dice_** (FD) invested in the spell.\
[Sum] is a number equal to the sum of rolled **_Magic Dice_** or **_Faith Dice_**.

## Spellcasting

**_Magic Dice_** or **_Faith Dice_** represent a magic user's 'energy' and the taxing nature of casting spells over a long period. When they run out of these, they cannot cast any more spells. Some equipment grants additional **_Magic Dice_**.

The **_Spell Attribute_** for **_Arcane Magic_** is **_INT_** and for **_Divine Magic_** it is **_WIS_**

To cast a spell, the spellcaster must test their **_Spell Attribute_** at DR12 + [Dice] If they pass, the spell is successfully cast. On a 1, they expend a Die. On a 20, no Dice can be consumed by the spell nor is there any chance of **_Arcane Feedback_** (for **_Arcane Magic_**).

For each 6, you can choose to roll an additional Dice, even beyond your skills or gear. The total rolled Dice can never exceed 4.

Play out the effects of the chosen spell.

For every 1-2 rolled they reduce the number of Dice they have available by 1. The Dice consumed first are those given by equipment.

After roughly 8 hours rest (**_[Respite](#resting)_**), the number of Dice a **_Character_** has refreshes to its maximum.
